Third-party notices
===================

This file records the third-party inputs the release reads or depends on, with
the licence and the source each one declares. Nothing here is redistributed with
the release: the cohorts are fetched from the services below, and the packages are
installed from their own distribution channels.

Datasets
--------

TCGA-KIRC (training resource)
  Source: National Cancer Institute Genomic Data Commons
  URL: https://portal.gdc.cancer.gov/projects/TCGA-KIRC
  Access: open tier, no application step declared on the project record
  Licence: not declared by the source
  Content check: the project record returns the accession TCGA-KIRC, the name
  "Kidney Renal Clear Cell Carcinoma" and the primary site Kidney.

CPTAC-ccRCC (external cohort, proteomic arm)
  Source: Proteomic Data Commons, study PDC000127
  URL: https://pdc.cancer.gov/pdc/study/PDC000127
  Access: open download
  Licence: the matched imaging collection is released under CC BY 4.0; the
  proteomic arm declares no licence of its own
  Content check: the study record describes clear cell renal cell carcinoma with
  global proteome and phosphoproteome over the CPTAC discovery cohort.

CPTAC-ccRCC imaging collection (histology)
  Source: The Cancer Imaging Archive
  URL declared in the manuscript: https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/cptac-ccrcc/
  DOI declared in the manuscript: 10.7937/k9/tcia.2018.oblamn27
  Access: released openly, version 14, released 2025-07-07, CC BY 4.0 as declared
  Note: this host is not reachable from the environment where this release was
  assembled, so the version, release date and licence are transcribed from the
  manuscript's Data Availability Statement rather than re-fetched. No imaging data
  is redistributed here.

GSE29609 (external cohort)
  Source: Gene Expression Omnibus
  URL: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE29609
  Access: freely downloadable, no access restriction
  Licence: not declared by the source
  Content check: the series record is titled "Clear-cell renal cell carcinomas
  tumors" and reports 39 samples, matching the cohort size in the manuscript.

E-MTAB-1980 (external cohort)
  Source: ArrayExpress / BioStudies
  URL: https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/arrayexpress/studies/E-MTAB-1980
  Access: openly released since 2013-10-16
  Licence: not declared by the source
  Content check: the study record is titled "ccRCC_expression" and describes gene
  expression in clear cell renal cell carcinoma for 101 samples.

Comparators named in the manuscript's main comparison
-----------------------------------------------------

The comparator rows of Table 1 are independent implementations written for this
release under one shared protocol. The methods they stand for are named in the
manuscript's reference list; no third-party implementation code is vendored here.
Uncertainty-aware survival analysis, sparse and hierarchical transformers for
whole-slide survival, cell-aware transformers, linear-attention multiple-instance
learning and dense mixture-of-experts transformers are all re-expressed from their
published descriptions rather than copied.
